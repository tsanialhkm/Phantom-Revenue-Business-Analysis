# KPI Dictionary, version 1.8

**Last updated:** 15 October 2020  
**Owner:** Business Operations

## Revenue

Total value of invoice lines recognized in the reporting period, excluding tax.

**Formula:** quantity multiplied by unit price

**Reporting date:** invoice date

**Known limitation:** The dictionary does not specify how reversals, cancellations, credits, fees, or manual adjustments should be presented. Finance and Product owners must agree treatment before the next revision.
