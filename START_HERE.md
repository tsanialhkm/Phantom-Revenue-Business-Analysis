# Investigation Brief

## Your role

Junior Business Analyst, Alder & Finch Retail

## Business situation

The monthly Product dashboard and the Finance reconciliation report show different revenue for August 2011. Both teams say their number comes from the invoice system.

Senior management needs one defensible figure for the monthly business review.

## Objective

Determine why the two reported figures differ, quantify every material component of the gap, and recommend which revenue definition management should use going forward.

## Required final answer

Your case closure memo must answer:

1. What amount did Product report?
2. What amount did Finance report?
3. Why do the figures differ?
4. Is either dataset incomplete or technically wrong?
5. Which KPI definition should management adopt, and why?
6. What control would prevent this disagreement from recurring?

## Rules

- Do not assume that a column name is a business definition.
- Keep at least two hypotheses open until evidence rejects one.
- Cite query outputs or evidence files for every important claim.
- You may use SQL, Python, Excel, or Power BI, but your result must be reproducible.
- Do not search for a public solution. This case was created specifically for this project.
